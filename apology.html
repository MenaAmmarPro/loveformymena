A MOMENT FOR HONEST WORDS

A little note.

Some things are better expressed with patience, honesty, and care.

🌷

Dear Mena,

If something I've done has hurt you, then I want to make room to understand how you feel — not assume, not explain it away, just listen.

I want these words to be honest and specific. If I made a mistake, I want to name what I did, recognize how it landed for you, and be clear about what I'll do differently.

You deserve sincerity, not just pretty words. I hope we can hold the difficult things gently — with kindness, respect, and an open mind, the way we deserve to.

With care, ♥

Some words are worth saying out loud too — and I mean these ones.
