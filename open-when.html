LETTERS FOR DIFFERENT DAYS

Open when…

Whenever you need one, open a little letter. There is one for every kind of day.

🌧 Open when you miss me

Close your eyes and count to five. I am right there, in the same quiet place you keep me. Distance is only geography; you are never far from me in the ways that matter.

Put your hand over your heart. That steady thump is me, still yours, still here. Miss me for a minute, then let me hold the rest.

😭 Open when you are sad

Hey. Whatever this is, you do not have to carry it perfectly. You are allowed to be tired, to be quiet, to be a little broken today. Nothing about that changes how I see you.

Breathe. Drink some water. Wrap yourself in something soft. Feelings are visitors, not residents. I am so proud of you for staying — let me carry the heavy part for a while.

🌙 Open when you cannot sleep

It is late, and the world has gone quiet just for you. Let your shoulders drop. There is nothing left to fix tonight; tomorrow can wait its turn.

Picture a calm, warm place: slow rain on a window, a light left on for you. I am thinking of you right now, soft and safe. Close your eyes. I will keep watch.

🎉 Open when you are happy

Look at you, glowing. I love the version of you that laughs too loud and forgets to be careful. This is the you I hope you get to be most days.

Save a little of this feeling somewhere you can find it later. And tell me everything — I want to be happy about it with you.

💪 Open when you need courage

You have walked through harder days than this one and come out kind on the other side. That was not luck. That was you.

Whatever is standing in front of you, you do not have to do it without fear. Do it scared. Do it slowly. Do it your way. I believe in you, loudly, even from here.

💕 Open when you just need a hug

Come here. Imagine my arms all the way around you, chin on your head, one long breath. You do not need to say anything.

You are held, you are wanted, and you are so, so loved — right this second, exactly as you are. Stay here a moment longer. That is all this one asks.
