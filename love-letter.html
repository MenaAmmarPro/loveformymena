WORDS FROM THE HEART

A letter for Mena.

Some feelings deserve more than a passing moment.

✦   ♥   ✦

Dear Mena,

From the first conversation we ever shared, something about you felt different — quieter, kinder, and a little bit like home. I wanted to give that feeling somewhere to live.

There are things that never quite fit inside a message. So I made this little corner instead: a place where the words can take their time, and where every small detail means exactly what I hoped it would.

I hope this brings a smile to your face. Every page here is a thought turned into something you can keep — a feeling, a memory, a wish that you always feel as appreciated as you truly are.

But the most important part of this letter is the part only honesty can fill. I want to share our real memories, say the things I have always meant to say, and let you know plainly: you matter to me, Mena.

With care and warmth,

Always, Ammar ♥

Take your time. There is no rush.
